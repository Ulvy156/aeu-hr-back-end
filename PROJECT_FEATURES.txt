HR MANAGEMENT SYSTEM - FEATURE LIST (SIMPLE EXPLANATION)
==========================================================
This file lists every feature currently built in this backend project,
explained in simple, easy-to-understand words.

1. LOGIN & AUTHENTICATION
--------------------------
Lets users log in with email and password to get a secure access token
(no passwords are sent again after login; the token proves who they are).
Includes logging out and checking "who am I" (current logged-in user info).

2. USER ACCOUNTS & ROLES
--------------------------
Manages the login accounts of every person in the system (admin, HR, CEO,
employee). Each user has exactly one role. Admins create, update, disable,
delete users, reset passwords, and assign access. HR can list/search users.

3. ROLES & PERMISSIONS
--------------------------
Controls what each type of user is allowed to do. There are 4 roles:
- Admin: user accounts, permissions, settings, backups, and administrative views
- HR: manages employees, attendance, leave approvals, payroll preparation,
  draft payroll deletion, recruitment, and announcements; no final payroll approval
- CEO: approves/rejects leave, payroll, and employee changes; views reports
- Employee: uses their own attendance, leave, payslips, profile
GM, Head, and Manager are organizational job levels, not access roles.
They default to Employee access. Job titles do not automatically grant
announcement, leave approval, or payroll permissions. Admin, HR, and CEO
roles are preserved when job levels change.
Permissions can be viewed and assigned to control access precisely.

4. DEPARTMENTS
--------------------------
Manage the list of company departments (e.g. Sales, IT, Finance) - create,
view, update, delete.

5. POSITIONS (JOB TITLES)
--------------------------
Manage job positions/titles (e.g. "HR Officer", "Accountant"), which can
be linked to a department.

6. EMPLOYEES
--------------------------
Manage employee profiles: name, contact info, department, position,
salary, photo, and linked login account. Each employee gets an
auto-generated ID like EMP-00001.

7. EMPLOYEE ORG CHART (HIERARCHY)
--------------------------
Shows the company's reporting structure as a tree - who reports to whom,
based on each employee's manager.

8. EMPLOYMENT HISTORY
--------------------------
A read-only career timeline for each employee, showing past changes to
department, position, salary, employment status, manager, and probation
end date. This history is created automatically when an official change
request is approved.

9. EMPLOYEE UPGRADE REQUESTS
--------------------------
A formal way for HR to propose important employee changes (promotion,
transfer, salary raise, status change, new manager) that must be approved
by the CEO before taking effect. Once approved, it updates the employee
record and adds an entry to Employment History.

10. ATTENDANCE
--------------------------
Employees clock in and out using their phone's GPS location, which the
system checks against the office location (must be within an allowed
distance). Features include:
- Clock in / clock out
- Morning clock-in is allowed from midnight through noon, including early
  arrivals before work starts. Afternoon clock-out is allowed from 13:00 onward;
  the lunch gap is blocked. Half-day boundaries are configurable. Early arrivals
  are present; clock-ins after the configured work start time are late.
- QR code-based attendance (generate and scan a QR code to clock in)
- Automatically detecting late arrivals
- At 9:30 a.m., flagging missing morning attendance as "Missing clock in".
  At 5:30 p.m., flagging missing afternoon attendance as "Missing clock out".
  If both are missing, the record shows "Missing both". These flags are for
  HR review and do not count as absence.
- At 11:00 p.m., checking both halves against recorded attendance and approved
  leave, then marking each unresolved half-day absent (0.5 day each).
- All automatic checks skip non-working days, active public holidays, dates
  outside employment, approved leave for the relevant half, and HR corrections.
  Pending leave does not excuse attendance. Repeated checks reuse daily records.
  Morning and afternoon checks do not downgrade finalized absences or past dates.
- Later GPS, QR, or HR proxy attendance clears the matching missing flag.
  Later approved leave clears flags or absence only for the covered time.
  Reading attendance summaries never changes attendance records.
- HR may mark an employee absent before 11 p.m. by correcting the individual
  record with a required reason. Automatic checks preserve that decision.
- HR can manually correct attendance records
- "Proxy clock" - HR can clock in/out on behalf of an employee if needed

11. LEAVE MANAGEMENT
--------------------------
Employees can request time off. Leave types include:
- Annual leave (18 days/year)
- Sick leave (7 days/year)
- Special leave (7 days/year)
- Maternity leave (90 days)
- Special sick leave (long-term illness, up to 180 days with reducing pay)
Every leave request needs two approvals: first HR, then the CEO ("dual
approval"). Employees can also cancel a leave request, and everyone can
check their current leave balance (how many days they have left).

12. PUBLIC HOLIDAYS
--------------------------
Manage the list of official company holidays. These days are excluded
from attendance lateness/absence checks and affect payroll calculations.

13. PAYROLL
--------------------------
Generates employee salaries automatically each pay period. The system:
- Calculates prorated salary (partial pay if someone joined/left mid-month)
- Applies tax deductions based on salary brackets
- Applies NSSF (social security) deductions
- Requires HR to generate and submit payroll, then CEO must approve or
  reject it before it's final

14. PAYSLIPS
--------------------------
Once payroll is approved, each employee gets a downloadable PDF payslip
showing their salary breakdown (earnings, deductions, net pay). Employees
can view/download their own; HR/Admin can view/download anyone's.

15. COMPANY SETTINGS
--------------------------
A single place to configure company-wide rules used across the system:
company name/logo/contact info, office GPS location and allowed clock-in
distance, working hours, working days, currency, and payroll day-rate.

16. DASHBOARD
--------------------------
Gives each role a quick summary screen after login, with different
information depending on whether you're Admin, HR, CEO, or Employee
(e.g. headcount, pending approvals, attendance summary).

17. REPORTS & EXCEL EXPORTS
--------------------------
Generates reports for payroll, attendance, and leave, viewable on screen
or exported as Excel files for record-keeping or offline analysis.

18. AUDIT LOGS
--------------------------
Keeps a permanent record of important actions taken in the system (who
did what, when, and what changed). Only Admin and CEO can view this log.
Used to track accountability and investigate issues.

19. MANUAL BACKUP
--------------------------
Allows an Admin to trigger a backup of the system's data and download it,
for safekeeping outside the live system.

20. ANNOUNCEMENTS
--------------------------
An internal company notice/bulletin board. HR creates announcements which
go through a workflow: Draft -> Pending Approval -> Published (or
Rejected) -> Archived. Announcements can be:
- Sent to everyone, a specific role, a specific department, or one person
- Attached with one file
- Tracked for which employees have read them
Announcement categories (e.g. "Policy", "Event") can also be managed.

21. RECRUITMENT
--------------------------
A simple hiring tracker with two parts:
- Vacancies: job openings, how many people are needed, how many are
  filled, and whether the position is open or closed
- Candidates: applicants for a vacancy, their CV, where they applied
  from, interview scheduling, pipeline status (e.g. applied, interviewed,
  hired/rejected)

==========================================================
NOTE: All calculations (attendance distance, late/absent detection, leave
balances, payroll amounts, tax, and proration) are always done by the
backend server, never trusted from the app/frontend, to keep the data
accurate and secure.
